# Tribulation

A first-person, PS2-style horror shooter built for mobile browsers.

**Play:** https://bondservant-of-christ.github.io/tribulation/

## Controls
- **Phone:** left thumb moves, drag the right side to look. Hold **AIM** to raise the weapon; sidearms fire when you let go (a steady hold hits harder), automatics fire while held.
- **Desktop:** click to capture the mouse. WASD to move, hold click to aim, R reload, Q or scroll wheel to swap, Esc to pause.

For the best experience on a phone, play in landscape. Adding the page to your home screen hides the browser bars.
